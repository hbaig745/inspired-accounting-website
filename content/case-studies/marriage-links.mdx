One of our oldest clients and going stronger than ever.

When they contacted us back in 2007 to say their accounting function was a mess, it couldn't have been more of an understatement. They were acutely aware they had a problem because they'd had a bookkeeper, an auditor to review the bookkeeper, an accountant to review that bookkeeper, and another auditor to audit that accountant.

We eliminated the confusion of all that unnecessary hassle. Within 6 months, we completely re-designed the systems to the manager's specifications and her assessments of the business units. When these were reported on, it became very clear what services were leaking money.

Today, it is one of the most successful centres of the organisation — providing a much-needed service in our community.
