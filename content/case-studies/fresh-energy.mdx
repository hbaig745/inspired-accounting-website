How does someone go from being an employee to running his own company, and looking to hit a £1 million turnover in the first 3 years? How did a pipe dream become a reality for our client?

Business owners need the support to do what they do best, and not be brought down in the nitty-gritty of accounting-based problems.

Our arrangement is simple. The client — in this case an engineer — continues to do what he does best, whilst we accountants solve his problems ahead of them happening. By communicating with our client, we were able to work together and find the best conclusion that matched his long-term goals and business objectives.
