For over a decade, one property investor had fallen out of step with HMRC. Correspondence had gone unanswered, self-assessment obligations had slipped, and — like many people who find themselves in this position — he simply didn't know where to start putting it right, or who he could trust to help.

## The problem

By the time we were introduced, he'd built a genuinely impressive property portfolio, generating around £500,000 a year in net income. He'd started with nothing — no formal education, no financial backing, just graft and good instincts for property. But the systems and support needed to keep pace with HMRC's requirements had never quite caught up with the business's success.

## What we did

We got to work rebuilding eleven years of financial history from the ground up. The records were scattered and unstructured, so our first job was simply to create order: gathering documentation, reconstructing each year's position, and calculating the tax, penalties and interest due. We then prepared and submitted a full voluntary disclosure to HMRC under their property income campaign.

HMRC initially queried the figures we submitted, so we brought in a specialist tax investigator to work the case through to resolution. The figures were subsequently accepted by HMRC in full, as complete and accurate.

## Where they are today

The result was more than a compliance fix. Eleven years of uncertainty lifted in one process, and our client came away with a clean, well-documented tax position — and the confidence to think beyond the immediate problem. Since then, we've helped him put a long-term structure in place for the business, with succession and inheritance planning for his children now underway.

To make sure the position stays clean going forward, we also introduced him to accounting software suited to his business and worked with him to set it up around how he actually operates — from day-to-day record-keeping to reporting ahead of each tax year. That system is now fully up and running, giving him a straightforward way to manage his own affairs year after year, with far less risk of ever falling behind again.

We're proud to have helped turn a decade of worry into a foundation for the next generation.
